## Full description (1683 of 4000 characters)
HookCalc puts the numbers that crane operators, riggers and lift supervisors need into one simple app. Big inputs, clear results, and a red STOP warning when a lift is not safe.

CALCULATORS
• Crane capacity: load, hook block and rigging as a % of the load chart, with a critical lift warning
• Sling tension: 1 to 4 legs, from sling length and height, sling angle, or an off-centre load
• Load weight: plates, bars, pipes and tanks with liquid, ISMB, HEB, IPE and W sections, volume or piece count
• Boom and radius: working radius, boom length needed, and clearance over obstacles
• Ground bearing: outrigger and crawler pressure, ground types, and the mat size you need
• Wind check: wind limit for large-area loads, Beaufort scale, wind force and load swing
• Centre of gravity: CG of combined loads and the share at two lift points or two cranes
• Parts of line: reeving needed and hook block check
• Unit converter: t, kg, lb, kN, kPa, t/m², psi, m/s, km/h, knots and more

LIFT PLAN
Collects your results with the job details and a pre-lift checklist. Share it on WhatsApp, copy it, or print it and save it as a PDF.

FIELD GUIDE
Crane hand signals, sling angle factors, hitch types, sling colour code, grade 80 chain and shackle WLL tables, load chart tips, wind scale and power line distances.

MADE FOR SITE WORK
• English, Hindi and Tamil
• Metric (t, m) and imperial (lb, ft) units
• Works without signal after the first start
• Light and dark theme
• No ads, no account, no data collection

IMPORTANT
HookCalc is a calculation aid. Always use the manufacturer's load chart, the rigging tags and your site rules, and have a competent person plan and supervise every lift.
