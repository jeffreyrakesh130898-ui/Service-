HookCalc privacy policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

HookCalc is a crane and rigging calculator. This page explains what happens to your information when you use the app or this website.

What we collect

Nothing. HookCalc has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not collect, receive, sell or share any personal data.

What stays on your device

The values you enter, your lift plan, checklist and settings are saved only in your device's browser storage, so they are still there next time. They leave your device only when you choose to share them, for example with the Share, WhatsApp, Copy or Print buttons. You can delete everything at any time in Settings, Delete all data, or by uninstalling the app.

Hosting

The app files are hosted on GitHub Pages. When your device downloads them, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver the files, as described in the GitHub privacy statement.

Children

HookCalc is a tool for professionals and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
